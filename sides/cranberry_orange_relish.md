# Mom's Cranberry-Orange Relish

Adapted from Mom's recipe via Mary Roelofs (2008).

## Equipment

* Food processor

## Ingredients

* 1 bag fresh cranberries
* 1 large seedless orange (cut into chunks; use 2 if smaller than a tennis ball)
* 1 cup granulated sugar

## Directions

1. Place cranberries in the food processor and pulse until blended/finely chopped. Transfer to a bowl.
2. Cut up the orange (remove any seeds if needed). Place orange chunks in the food processor and process/frappe until chopped, keeping some texture.
3. Add the processed orange to the bowl with the cranberries.
4. Stir in the sugar until well mixed. The mixture should remain pleasantly chunky.
5. Transfer to an airtight container and refrigerate for 48 hours before serving to let the flavors meld and sweeten.
