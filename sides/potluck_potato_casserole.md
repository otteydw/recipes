# Potluck Potato Casserole

Adapted from Cooking Light magazine via Tamara Mathew.

## Preheat

350°F

## Yield

10 servings (9 x 13-inch baking dish)

## Ingredients

* 1 (30 oz) package frozen hash browns, thawed (such as Ore-Ida)
* 1 1/2 cups sour cream (or reduced-fat / fat-free)
* 1 1/4 cups (5 oz) finely shredded sharp cheddar cheese
* 1 (10.75 oz) can cream of chicken soup (such as Campbell's Healthy Request)
* 1/2 cup chicken broth
* 5 teaspoons butter, melted
* 2 tablespoons minced fresh onion (or 1 white onion, chopped to taste)
* 1 to 2 red bell peppers, chopped (optional, to taste)
* 1/2 teaspoon freshly ground black pepper
* 1/4 teaspoon salt
* 1/8 teaspoon ground red pepper (cayenne)
* Cooking spray
* 1 cup coarsely crushed cornflakes
* 2 tablespoons fresh parsley, chopped

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F. Coat a 9 x 13-inch baking dish with cooking spray.
2. In a large mixing bowl, combine hash browns, sour cream, cheddar cheese, cream of chicken soup, chicken broth, melted butter, onion, red pepper (if using), black pepper, salt, and cayenne pepper. Mix until thoroughly combined.
3. Spread mixture evenly into the prepared baking dish. (Note: Can be assembled ahead of time and refrigerated until ready to bake).
4. Sprinkle crushed cornflakes evenly across the top just before baking.
5. Bake at 350°F for 1 hour or until hot and bubbly.
6. Garnish with fresh chopped parsley before serving.
