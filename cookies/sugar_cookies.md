# Sugar Cookies

Classic rollout holiday sugar cookies that hold their shape. Adapted from Alton Brown on [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/alton-brown/sugar-cookies-recipe-1909614) and [altonbrown.com](https://altonbrown.com/recipes/simple-sugar-cookies-2-0/).

## Preheat

375°F (190°C)

## Yield

About 2 to 3 dozen cookies (depending on cutter size)

## Ingredients

* 3 cups (420 g) all-purpose flour
* 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
* 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt
* 1 cup (2 sticks / 227 g) unsalted butter, cool room temperature (60°F–65°F)
* 1 cup (200 g) granulated sugar
* 1 large egg
* 1 tablespoon whole milk
* Confectioners' (powdered) sugar, for rolling out dough

## Directions

1. In a medium bowl, whisk together the flour, baking powder, and kosher salt. Set aside.
2. In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment (or using a hand mixer), beat butter and sugar together on medium speed until smooth and creamy (do not over-whip or aerate excessively).
3. Add the egg and whole milk, mixing until fully incorporated.
4. Reduce mixer speed to low and gradually add the flour mixture, beating just until the dough comes together and no dry pockets remain.
5. Divide the dough into two equal portions, flatten each into a disk, and wrap tightly in plastic wrap. Refrigerate for at least 2 hours until firm.
6. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C) and line baking sheets with parchment paper.
7. Dust your work surface and rolling pin with confectioners' sugar (using powdered sugar instead of flour keeps the dough tender). Roll out one disk of dough to about 1/4-inch thickness.
8. Cut out shapes using cookie cutters and transfer to prepared baking sheets, leaving about 1 inch between cookies. Gather scraps, re-roll, and cut additional shapes.
9. Bake for 7 to 9 minutes, watching closely—the cookies should be cooked through with edges just barely beginning to turn golden.
10. Cool on the baking sheet for 2 to 3 minutes, then transfer to a wire cooling rack to cool completely before icing.
