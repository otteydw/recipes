# Sugar Cookies

Classic rollout holiday sugar cookies that hold their shape. Adapted from Alton Brown on [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/alton-brown/sugar-cookies-recipe-1909614) and [altonbrown.com](https://altonbrown.com/recipes/simple-sugar-cookies-2-0/).

## Preheat

375°F (190°C)

## Yield

About 2 to 3 dozen cookies (depending on cutter size)

## Ingredients

* 3 cups all-purpose flour
* 3/4 teaspoon baking powder
* 1/4 teaspoon table salt (or 1/2 teaspoon kosher salt)
* 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
* 1 cup granulated sugar
* 1 large egg, beaten
* 1 tablespoon milk
* Powdered (confectioners') sugar, for rolling out dough

## Directions

1. Sift together the flour, baking powder, and salt in a bowl. Set aside.
2. In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment, cream butter and sugar together until light in color and smooth (avoid over-whipping).
3. Add the beaten egg and milk, beating to combine.
4. Set mixer to low speed and gradually add the flour mixture, beating just until the dough pulls away from the sides of the bowl.
5. Divide dough in half, wrap each half in waxed paper or plastic wrap, and refrigerate for 2 hours.
6. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C). Line baking sheets with parchment paper or silicone mats.
7. Dust work surface and rolling pin with powdered sugar (using powdered sugar instead of flour keeps the dough tender and sweet).
8. Roll out dough to 1/4-inch thickness, checking underneath frequently to ensure it does not stick.
   * *Tip:* If the dough warms up and softens during rolling, place a cold cookie sheet on top for 10 minutes to re-chill.
9. Cut into desired shapes and arrange at least 1 inch apart on prepared baking sheets.
10. Bake for 7 to 9 minutes, rotating the cookie sheet halfway through, until edges are just beginning to turn golden brown.
11. Let rest on the baking sheet for 2 minutes after removing from oven, then transfer to a wire cooling rack to cool completely.
12. Serve as is or decorate with royal icing. Store in an airtight container for up to 1 week.
