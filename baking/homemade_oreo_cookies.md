# Homemade 'Oreo' Cookies

Rich chocolate sandwich cookies with a creamy vanilla filling. Adapted from Trois Pommes Patisserie (Brooklyn) for [Food Network Magazine](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/trois-pommes-patisserie/oreo-cookies-recipe-remix-1951559).

## Preheat

325°F (165°C)

## Yield

32 sandwich cookies (64 cookie wafers)

## Timing

* Prep time: 40 minutes
* Chilling time: 1 hour 20 minutes
* Cook time: 20 minutes

## Ingredients

### Dough

* 1 1/3 cups Dutch-process cocoa powder
* 1 1/2 cups all-purpose flour (plus more for dusting)
* 1/4 teaspoon salt
* 1 cup (2 sticks) unsalted butter, softened
* 2 cups granulated sugar
* 2 large eggs
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

### Filling

* 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, softened
* 1/2 cup vegetable shortening
* 3 cups confectioners' (powdered) sugar, sifted
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract

## Directions

1. **Make the dough:** Sift together the Dutch-process cocoa powder, flour, and salt in a large bowl.
2. In a stand mixer fitted with the paddle attachment (or using a hand mixer), cream the butter and sugar until fluffy. Add the eggs one at a time, then the vanilla extract, beating well after each addition.
3. Add the dry ingredients and mix just until incorporated, scraping the bottom and sides of the bowl with a rubber spatula.
4. **Roll and chill:** Divide dough into 2 equal portions. Place one piece between 2 lightly floured sheets of parchment paper and roll into a 1/4-inch-thick rectangle. Repeat with the second piece. Refrigerate both parchment-wrapped rectangles until firm, at least 1 hour (or up to several days).
5. **Cut and second chill:** Using a 2-inch round cookie cutter, cut dough into 64 circles (dough scraps can be rerolled once). Place cookies about 2 inches apart on ungreased baking sheets and chill in refrigerator for 20 minutes.
6. **Bake:** Preheat oven to 325°F (165°C). Bake cookies until set and slightly darker around the edges, about 20 minutes. Transfer to wire racks and cool completely.
7. **Make the filling:** In a mixer bowl, cream the softened butter and shortening until light and fluffy. Beat in the sifted confectioners' sugar and vanilla extract until smooth.
8. **Assemble:** Flip half (32) of the cookies flat-side up and top each with 1 level tablespoon of filling. Place the remaining cookies on top and press gently to spread the filling toward the edges.
