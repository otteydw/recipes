# Sourdough Starter Maintenance

Routine care and batch preparation ratios for sourdough starter.

## Starter Refresh (Glass Jar)

### Ingredients
* Sourdough starter: up to 100g
* Whole wheat flour: equal weight to starter (e.g., 100g)
* Room temperature water: equal weight to starter (e.g., 100g)

### Method
1. Place starter into a clean glass jar.
2. Mix in equal parts wheat flour and lukewarm water until smooth.
3. Cover loosely with a lid and let rest until bubbly and active.

---

## Two-Loaf Batch Preparation (Bowl)

### Ingredients
* Sourdough starter: 250g
* Wheat flour: 250g
* Water: 250g

### Method
1. Combine starter, flour, and water in a large mixing bowl.
2. Stir thoroughly until fully hydrated.
3. Cover and allow to ferment until bubbly and approximately doubled in volume before incorporating into your final dough.
