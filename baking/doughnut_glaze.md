# Doughnut Glaze

Quick warm vanilla glaze for dipping doughnuts or drizzling over sweet quick breads. Adapted from Alton Brown on [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/alton-brown/doughnut-glaze-recipe-1943024) and [altonbrown.com](https://altonbrown.com/recipes/doughnut-glaze/).

## Timing

* Prep time: 5 minutes
* Cook time: 3 minutes

## Yield

Enough glaze for 20 to 25 doughnuts (or a generous drizzle over banana bread)

## Ingredients

### Full Batch

* 1/4 cup whole milk
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 2 cups confectioners' (powdered) sugar

### Half Batch

* 1/8 cup (2 tablespoons) whole milk
* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
* 1 cup confectioners' (powdered) sugar

## Directions

1. Combine the milk and vanilla extract in a medium saucepan and heat gently over low heat until warm.
2. Sift the confectioners' sugar into the milk mixture.
3. Whisk slowly until smooth and completely combined.
4. Remove the glaze from the heat and set the saucepan over a bowl of warm water to keep it fluid.
5. Dip warm doughnuts into glaze (or spoon over baked goods) and rest on a wire rack set over a baking sheet for 5 minutes to set before serving.

## Notes

* **Banana Bread Glaze:** Excellent when drizzled over freshly cooled [Banana Bread](banana_bread.md).
