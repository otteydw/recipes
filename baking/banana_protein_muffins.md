# Flourless Banana Protein Muffins

Moist, naturally sweetened flourless protein muffins. Adapted from Matt's Fit Chef.

## Preheat

350°F (180°C)

## Yield

9 muffins

## Timing

* Prep time: 5 minutes
* Cook time: 20-25 minutes

## Ingredients

* 2 large ripe bananas
* 1 large egg
* 1/4 cup (60 g) peanut butter (or almond butter)
* 1/2 cup + 2 tablespoons (60 g) vanilla protein powder
* 1 teaspoon baking powder
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract (optional, if using unflavored protein powder)
* 1 to 3 tablespoons milk of choice (as needed if batter is too thick)
* 3 tablespoons chocolate chips (plus extra for topping)

## Directions

1. Preheat oven to 350°F (180°C). Line a muffin tin with 9 paper liners and lightly grease them to prevent sticking.
2. In a large bowl, thoroughly mash the ripe bananas.
3. Add the egg and peanut butter, whisking until well combined.
4. Sift in the protein powder and baking powder. Whisk until smooth (do not overmix). If the batter seems too thick or dry, stir in 1 to 3 tablespoons of milk until scoopable.
5. Fold in the chocolate chips.
6. Divide the batter evenly between the 9 muffin cups and top with extra chocolate chips if desired.
7. Bake for 20 to 25 minutes, or until a toothpick inserted into the center comes out clean.
8. Let cool completely on a wire rack before serving.
