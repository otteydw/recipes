# Mint Wine Mojito

A refreshing wine mojito using sweet mint wine and frozen limeade. Adapted from [Shade Mountain Winery](https://www.shademountainwinery.com/).

## Ingredients

* 6 oz can frozen limeade concentrate
* 1/2 bottle Shade Mountain Mint Wine (or sweet mint wine)
* 1/4 cup water
* Crushed ice
* Dash of rum (optional)
* Fresh lime slices, for garnish

## Directions

1. In a pitcher or blender, mix together the frozen limeade, water, and crushed ice until the ice reaches a coarse slush.
2. Stir in the mint wine and optional dash of rum.
3. Pour into glasses, garnish with fresh lime slices, and serve cold.
