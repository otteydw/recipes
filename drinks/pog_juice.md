# POG Juice (Passion Orange Guava)

From Tamara Mathew's Christmas brunch menu.

## Ingredients

* 1 part orange juice
* 1 part passion fruit juice
* 1 part guava juice

## Directions

1. In a large pitcher, combine equal parts orange juice, passion fruit juice, and guava juice.
2. Stir thoroughly and chill before serving over ice.
