# Pumpkin Pie Martini

A festive dessert cocktail with a spiced sugar rim. Adapted from Sandra Lee on [Food Network](https://www.foodnetwork.com/recipes/sandra-lee/pumpkin-pie-martini-recipe-1922472).

> [!NOTE]
> **Status:** Untried / To-Try

## Timing

* Prep time: 10 minutes
* Inactive cooling time: 30 minutes
* Cook time: 5 minutes

## Yield

4 servings

## Ingredients

### Spiced Simple Syrup & Rim

* 1/2 cup brown sugar
* 2 teaspoons pumpkin pie spice (divided: 1 tsp for syrup, 1 tsp for rim)
* 1/2 cup water
* 2 tablespoons granulated sugar

### Cocktail Base

* 2 cups half-and-half
* 2 tablespoons pumpkin puree
* 1/2 teaspoon vanilla extract
* Ice cubes
* 4 oz (1/2 cup) vanilla vodka
* 1 liter sparkling water

## Directions

1. **Make spiced syrup:** In a small saucepan over medium heat, combine brown sugar, 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice, and 1/2 cup water. Stir until sugar dissolves, then bring to a boil. Remove from heat and cool for 30 minutes. (Syrup can be stored in the refrigerator for up to 2 weeks).
2. **Rim the glasses:** On a shallow plate, mix granulated sugar and the remaining 1 teaspoon pumpkin pie spice. Pour 2 tablespoons of the cooled simple syrup onto a second plate. Dip the rims of 4 martini glasses into syrup, then into spiced sugar to coat. Set aside.
3. **Mix cocktail base:** In a large measuring cup or bowl, whisk together half-and-half, pumpkin puree, vanilla extract, and half of the prepared simple syrup until well blended.
4. **Shake and serve:** Pour base mixture into an ice-filled pitcher or shaker. Add vanilla vodka and stir well. Pour into prepared martini glasses and top each with a splash of sparkling water.
