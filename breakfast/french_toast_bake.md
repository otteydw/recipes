# French Toast Bake

An overnight baked French toast casserole with a brown sugar butter crust. From Tamara Mathew's Christmas brunch recipes.

## Preheat

350°F

## Yield

9 x 13-inch baking dish

## Ingredients

* 1/2 cup (1 stick) unsalted butter, melted
* 1 cup brown sugar
* 1 loaf thickly sliced bread (Texas toast or Maier's Italian bread)
* 4 large eggs
* 1 1/2 cups milk
* 1 teaspoon vanilla extract
* Ground cinnamon, for dusting
* Powdered sugar, for dusting
* Warm maple syrup, for serving

## Directions

1. Melt butter in microwave and stir in brown sugar until well mixed.
2. Pour butter/sugar mixture into the bottom of a 9 x 13-inch baking dish and spread evenly.
3. In a bowl, beat eggs, milk, and vanilla extract.
4. Lay a single layer of sliced bread in the baking dish.
5. Spoon half of the egg mixture evenly over the bread layer.
6. Sprinkle cinnamon over the bread layer.
7. Add a second layer of sliced bread.
8. Spoon remaining egg mixture evenly over the top.
9. Cover and refrigerate overnight.
10. Preheat oven to 350°F.
11. Bake covered for 30 minutes, then uncover and bake for another 15 minutes (45 minutes total).
12. Dust with powdered sugar and cinnamon, and serve warm with maple syrup.
