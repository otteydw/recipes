# Sweet Pickle Relish

A classic sweet pickle relish for water bath canning.

## Yield

About 6 pints

## Ingredients

### Vegetables & Brine

* 1 quart cucumbers, chopped
* 2 cups onion, chopped
* 1 cup green bell pepper, chopped
* 1 cup red bell pepper, chopped (or substitute additional green bell pepper)
* 1/4 cup canning salt
* Cold water (enough to cover)

### Pickling Liquid & Spices

* 3 cups sugar
* 2 cups cider vinegar
* 1 tablespoon celery seed
* 1 tablespoon mustard seed

## Directions

1. **Brine vegetables:** Combine chopped cucumbers, onion, and peppers in a large bowl. Sprinkle with canning salt and cover with cold water. Let stand for 2 hours.
2. **Drain:** Drain thoroughly in a colander, pressing out any excess water.
3. **Boil syrup:** In a large pot, combine sugar, cider vinegar, celery seed, and mustard seed. Bring to a boil.
4. **Simmer:** Add the well-drained vegetables to the pot and simmer for 10 minutes.
5. **Pack jars:** Prepare canning jars and heat water in your canner. Pack the hot relish into hot jars, leaving 1/4-inch headspace.
6. **Process:** Wipe jar rims clean, apply two-piece canning lids, and process in a boiling water bath canner according to standard canning directions (typically 10 minutes for pints, adjusted for altitude).
