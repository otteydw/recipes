# Wilton Buttercream Icing

Classic crusting decorator buttercream for piping and decorating cakes and cookies. Adapted from [Wilton](https://www.wilton.com/buttercream-frosting/WLRECIP-41.html).

## Yield

About 2 1/2 cups icing

## Ingredients

* 1 cup solid vegetable shortening
* 1 teaspoon flavoring (clear vanilla, almond, or butter flavor)
* 7 to 8 teaspoons milk or water
* 1 lb confectioners' (powdered) sugar (about 4 cups)
* 1 tablespoon meringue powder (see notes)
* Pinch of salt (optional)

## Directions

1. In a large mixing bowl (or stand mixer), cream together the vegetable shortening, flavoring, and milk/water.
2. Add the confectioners' sugar, meringue powder (if using), and optional salt.
3. Mix on medium speed until all ingredients are thoroughly incorporated.
4. Blend for an additional minute or so until smooth and creamy.

## Variations

### Chocolate Buttercream

* Add 3/4 cup cocoa powder (or 3 oz unsweetened chocolate squares, melted).
* Add an additional 1 to 2 tablespoons water or milk.
* Mix until fully incorporated and creamy.

## Notes

* **Meringue Powder:** Helps the buttercream form a delicate crust, making it ideal for smooth finishes and sharp piped details. Omit if egg allergies are present.
* **Color:** Using all shortening and clear extracts produces a bright snow-white icing that takes food coloring cleanly.
